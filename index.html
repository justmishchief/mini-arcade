About Mini Arcade

Mini Arcade is a nostalgic collection of classic desktop, sports, and childhood tabletop games crafted directly for modern browsers with real-time multiplayer room codes and tactile physics.

Whether you want to play solo against tactical bots, share a device with a friend, or connect instantly with someone across devices via a 4-letter code, everything runs seamlessly with zero downloads.

🔑

Online Rooms

Generate clean 4-letter room codes to play live peer-to-peer multiplayer matches with friends across phones, tablets, or computers.

🏆

Global Leaderboard

Compete, win matches, and climb global score ranks with real-time stats syncing directly to your personalized arcade profile.

💬

Community Forum

An open space to discuss arcade strategies, drop gameplay feedback, and request new classic games to be built.
