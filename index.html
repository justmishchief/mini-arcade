About Mini Arcade

Mini Arcade is a nostalgic collection of classic desktop, sports, and childhood tabletop games crafted directly for your browser with real-time multiplayer room codes and tactile physics.

Whether you want to play solo against tactical bots, share a device with a friend, or connect instantly with someone across rooms via a 4-letter code, everything runs seamlessly with zero downloads.

This arcade is actively evolving with more games, leaderboards, and features on the way.
